# Requirements: global mineral plant workspace

This product is a read-only mineral plant visualization and engineering workspace. The detailed visual and interaction contract is specified in [plant-workbench-sdd.md](../../plant-workbench-sdd.md); release tests are listed in [plant-workbench-tdd.md](../../plant-workbench-tdd.md). Each requirement below has an executable regression gate.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| WP-001 | THE application SHALL open on a global mine-to-plant overview and expose separate 2D site plan, process schematic, 3D plant, trends and CAOS engine views. | `frontend/src/App.test.tsx::adversarial: the plant workspace cannot collapse all areas into one diagram mode` |
| WP-002 | THE area navigator SHALL open a distinct detail model for mine extraction, crushing and stockpiling, grinding, flotation, leaching/SX/EW, thickening/filtration, water balance, tailings and utilities. | `frontend/src/workbench/areas.test.ts::adversarial: every required area has its own non-empty detail graph` |
| WP-003 | EACH authored physical asset SHALL belong to exactly one named process area; an area detail SHALL distinguish core equipment from connected equipment outside its boundary. | `frontend/src/workbench/areas.test.ts::adversarial: assets cannot be duplicated or left outside area ownership` |
| WP-004 | EACH area view SHALL show its incoming and outgoing boundary streams with explicit endpoints and keep adjacent physical equipment outside the area's core model. | `frontend/src/workbench/areas.test.ts::adversarial: neighboring equipment stays boundary context outside an area core` |
| WP-005 | THE water view SHALL include every positive `water_m3_h` contribution, including slurry and solution streams, and SHALL not claim balance closure without complete site boundaries. | `frontend/src/workbench/areas.test.ts::adversarial: slurry and solution water contributions cannot disappear from balance routes` |
| WP-006 | A live observation SHALL retain its asset and tag identity, engineering unit, quality and monotonic delivery; bad, stale, incompatible or out-of-order data SHALL not be presented as valid. | `frontend/src/workbench/model.test.ts::adversarial: refuses wrong asset, unit and older delivery without overwriting history` |
| WP-007 | AN imported 3D model SHALL use explicit known asset mappings, reject external resources and preserve stable graph identity. | `frontend/src/workbench/scene.test.ts::adversarial: external resources and unknown mappings are refused` |
| WP-008 | AN unknown area identifier SHALL fail explicitly and SHALL not fall back to the global graph. | `frontend/src/workbench/areas.test.ts::adversarial: an unknown area cannot silently resolve to the global graph` |
| WP-009 | SELECTING an area from a global representation SHALL open that area's distinct representation while preserving the selected view mode and synchronizing URL, equipment and area summary. | `frontend/src/App.test.tsx::adversarial: opening an area preserves the selected site, schematic or 3D representation` |
| WB-014 | THE App SHALL open Focus mode for every simulation case or surface view and preserve its case/view/group/sub-view on exit; the gate runner SHALL require the Focus check and refuse arguments that redirect the measured route. | `frontend/src/gate-script.test.ts::adversarial: arguments that redirect what G18 measures are refused while Focus is required (the review plant, WB-014)` |
| WB-015 | THE product SHALL declare its App kind and Focus setting; invalid kinds, workbench waivers, malformed waiver reasons or SDD mismatches SHALL fail the SDD guard and gate runner. | `tests/adversarial/test_app_kind.py::test_an_unknown_app_value_fails` and `scripts/check_sdd.py` |
