# Method evidence and scientific manuscripts

OreBridge names its reader-facing methods directly: engineering thresholds, weighted reconciliation, Huber projection, causal histogram prediction and experimental transport-aware coherence. Internal design categories remain in the SDD and requirement metadata. Citation of a paper does not mean its algorithm was implemented; GDN is prior work, whereas HGB is genuinely fitted and compared here.

The current experiment tests a fixed-ratio dry-solids prior with 11 steady-state factors. It does not dynamically fuse all 14 visual engines, estimate tank inventory derivatives or establish physical root causes. Missingness, quality rejection and numerical estimation remain separate. A negative result is retained: the no-topology ablation has higher recall and precision than the combined TASC formulation.

The technical report documents the connected visual architecture, source integrations, equations, cases, numerical resolution, provenance and limits. A separate method manuscript examines the localization failure and paired held-out comparisons. Each must cite exact measured results and current source methods; neither describes the synthetic plant as a field evaluation. Manuscript records declare their own versions, identifiers and CC BY 4.0 license, separate from MIT software.

Product metadata lists the published concept identifiers and covered proposal. `check_manuscripts.py --release --zenodo` verifies actual records and versions. Publishing a report does not establish peer review, field efficacy or owner acceptance. Full software checks and served-artifact verification remain separate release evidence.
